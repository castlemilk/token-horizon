## Privacy and limits

All people, organizations, addresses, costs and counts in the wireframes and proposed payload are synthetic. The bundle contains no observed personal usage values, private prompts, production invitation tokens, credentials or browser screenshots. Raw source and connector responses are not bundled.

No authentication or management mutation was performed. Repository tests, the native app, live mobile rendering and assistive-technology workflows were not run. The 27 acceptance tests are proposed checks, not passing test results. The report PDF has not been certified as a tagged accessible PDF; the Markdown source and image descriptions provide alternate review formats.

No shared repository, issue, PR, deployment, public page, setting or permission was changed. No invitation or third-party message was sent.
