At a glance

One row per model configuration in the current scope and model group, sorted by all-trials score. Configurations that cover at least 80% of the scope’s tasks come first; the sentences above the table compare only those, and the others sit under their own sub-heading. Cost per attempt covers every iteration billed, failed and truncated ones included. Median seconds per completed iteration are measured over completed iterations only.

Findings

Computed by the data build for the scope and model group you choose. Each states its figure and unit, and its sample: the configurations used and the iterations behind the number. A finding names only configurations that cover at least 80% of the scope’s tasks. The answer above the findings is computed from the same numbers.

Score against cost

Each dot is one model configuration with a measured cost per attempt. Height is the all-trials score; the whisker is its 95% bootstrap interval where there are enough iteration values. Only models with a positive per-token price are plotted; the others are named in the notes and the table view. Both axes are zoomed to the data: the score axis may not start at zero, and the cost axis is logarithmic.

Model by task

Each cell is the all-trials score of one model on one task. Rows: models covering at least 80% of the tasks in scope come first, then by the mean over the tasks each model ran, with its coverage under the mean. A hatched cell was not run; a cell with a cross had every iteration fail; a cell marked T had iterations truncated at the output cap, which count as zero. A cell marked S stopped at a provider spend limit or quota, and is counted over the iterations that ran.

Reasoning effort

For each base model with two or more effort levels: score with its 95% interval, cost per attempt and median seconds per completed iteration, one chart each, on the same effort axis. Each chart has its own scale. A base model is compared only over the tasks that every one of its effort levels ran in the current scope; the count and the list are under its charts. A level with truncated iterations is marked T on each chart, and the count is in the note under its charts. A level with a cell stopped at a provider spend limit or quota is marked S and counted over the iterations that ran.

Reliability

One row per model. Each dot is one completed iteration's score on a 0 to 100 axis. Failed iterations are counted at the left of each row, and the right column counts completed over requested iterations.

Speed

Medians over the tasks in scope. Each task contributes its record-level mean per completed iteration. Timings are measured at the model endpoint (an API or a local runtime). Output speed is output tokens divided by the time after the first visible token, where output tokens include any reasoning tokens the provider bills. Agent command-line routes are left out, because their first-output time is not a token time.

What failed

Covers the tasks of the current scope: the headline tasks, or the horizon tasks in the horizon scope. The smoke tier has no checks matrix. Each cell is the share of iterations with a recorded result for that check that passed. A dash means the cell has no named check rows; truncated means the cell hit the output cap and has none. Within each task, the checks that fail most come first.

Gallery

Each thumbnail is the first of two captured screenshots of a model's artifact on a task of the current scope. Selecting one opens both captures with the check results, the cost per attempt and the timings. Nothing is run: the captures are stored images.

Full results

Every summary for the current scope and models. Select a column heading to sort. Not measured means the record did not carry the value.
