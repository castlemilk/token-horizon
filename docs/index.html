01 — The pieces

What you get.

One daemon, one database, every number in the same place — from the first token of the day to the quota reset next week.

01Tracking

Every request counted as it happens. Tokens and cost per model, per tool, per session, on cloud vendors and your own GPU. History survives restarts.

02Limits

Every plan quota and rate window on one screen, with reset times. You see the ceiling before you hit it.

03Leaderboards & Teams

Opt-in rankings across today, 7 days, all time, and streaks. Group your team to compare totals on the same board.

04Accounts

Sign in once and publish from any machine you run. Vendors with more than one login stay tracked separately.

05Privacy

Everything runs on your machine and meters need your permission first. Prompts are never logged and nothing is shared until you say so.
