Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-20

The short version

Token Horizon is local first. The app reads provider session files and quota endpoints directly from your machine, serves its dashboard over loopback (127.0.0.1), and stores everything in a local database. No account is required, and nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly connect it to a cloud server.

What stays on your machine

  • Token usage events, costs, and per-model breakdowns, measured by loopback request meters or imported from local tool session files.
  • Provider quota snapshots, fetched with credentials that never leave your machine.
  • System telemetry (CPU, memory, disk, network), retained in bounded in-memory windows only.

If you connect a cloud server

Cloud sync is optional, off until you sign in, and works with a server you control or one you choose to trust. When enabled, the daemon pushes usage events and quota snapshots associated with your handle. The server records the connecting IP address to show a country flag on the public leaderboard. You can disconnect at any time, which stops all further sync.

What we never collect

  • Prompt or response content. Meters measure token counts and timings, not text.
  • Analytics or crash telemetry to third parties. There is no tracking SDK in the app.
  • Provider credentials. They are read from your local credential stores and never transmitted to us.
